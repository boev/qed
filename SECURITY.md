# Security

Report suspected security issues privately to contact@web3-energy.com with subject "Security report". For content, issuer, or certificate takedown requests, contact contact@web3-energy.com with subject "Takedown request".

Please include the affected route or certificate ID, a concise description, reproduction steps, and a safe contact address. Do not include secrets or personal data in an initial report.

QED aims to acknowledge reports within seven days and will coordinate disclosure within a 90-day window from the initial report. The window may be extended when a fix or coordinated disclosure requires it.
